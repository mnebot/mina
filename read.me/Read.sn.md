# Mubatsiri wemumba wakangwara wemitauro yakawanda

Chirongwa ichi chinotsanangura kuvakwa kwemidziyo yemumba inoshanda munzvimbo imwe chete paRaspberry Pi, pamwe nemubatsiri wezwi anonzwisisa mitauro yakawanda.

## Kwakabva zita rokuti Mina

**Mina** rinobva pashoko rekare rechiGerman *minna*, rinoreva “ndangariro”, “rudo” kana “chinhu chinoyeukwa”. Zita iri rinowanikwawo mune mimwe mitauro: muchiPersian, *mīnā* rine chekuita ne“chiedza” kana “girazi rebhuruu”; muchiJapanese, *mina* rinoreva “vose” kana kuti “tose pamwe chete”.

Tsananguro idzi dzinomirira **ndangariro dzakagoveranwa uye kubatana kwevanhu**, zvinokosha zvinoenderana nemubatsiri anotsigira upenyu hwezuva nezuva mumba.

Pakunzwika, Mina izita rakapfava asi rakajeka. Rinodudzwa sezvikamu `Mi-na`, uye haringanyanyi kuvhiringidzwa nemashoko anowanzoshandiswa mumba.

## Chinangwa

Kugadzira hurongwa hwemumba hwakavanzika, hwakavimbika uye hunokurumidza, hunobvumira kudzora midziyo yemumba nemirayiro yezwi pasina kusungirwa kushandisa mabasa ari mugore remakombiyuta.

Marongerwo acho anosanganisa:

- Kugadzirisa mashoko mumba kuitira mirayiro yemumba uye kuchengetedza zvakavanzika.
- Ungwaru hwekombiyuta hwemuno hunoshandisa Ollama kupindura mibvunzo yakajairika.
- Mienzaniso yemugore remakombiyuta yemibvunzo yakaoma uye mashoko achangobva kubuda.

## Midziyo

- Raspberry Pi 5 ine RAM ye8 GB ndiyo inokurudzirwa. Inongotsigira zvishoma kusvika paRaspberry Pi 3B+.
- Dhisiki reNVMe SSD risingashandisi kadhi reSD. Kutsigira kadhi reSD kune muganhu.
- Maikorofoni nechinoridza USB zvinogona kutumira nokugamuchira inzwi.
- Midziyo yemumba inoenderana neZigbee, Matter, Philips Hue kana Wi-Fi yemuno.
- Kutonhodza kunoshanda kudzivirira kuderera kwesimba nekuda kwekupisa.

## Marongerwo ehurongwa

1. Home Assistant OS inotarisira midziyo nemabasa anoitwa otomatiki.
2. openWakeWord inoona shoko rekumutsa mubatsiri mumba.
3. Whisper inonyora mirayiro mumutauro uri kushandiswa ichishandisa Wyoming.
4. Chinogadzirisa chinangwa chinoita mirayiro yemumba munharaunda.
5. Piper inogadzira mhinduro dzezwi muchiCatalan.
6. Ollama inopindura mibvunzo yakajairika isina kutumira mashoko kuindaneti.
7. Ungwaru hwekombiyuta huri mugore hunongogamuchira mibvunzo yakasarudzwa nemushandisi pachena, inoda AI yepamusoro.

## Zvinhu zvakarongwa

- Kudzora marambi, masoketi, midziyo yekudziisa kana kutonhodza, maketeni nemimwe midziyo nezwi.
- Mamwe mazita mumitauro yakawanda.
- Nguva dzinoverengwa nokudzorwa nezwi.
- Kubvunza nezvemamiriro ekunze emunharaunda.
- Kuridza nokudzora mimhanzi.
- Imwe nzira yakavanzika inoshandisa muenzaniso weOllama.
- Kuziva munhu kubva pazwi rake.
- Kuchengeta mamiriro ehurukuro kwenguva refu.
- Kutumira mubvunzo kumuenzaniso weAI wegore kana zvichisarudzwa.
- Makopi ekuchengetedza uye kuongorora mashandiro ehurongwa.

## Misimboti yekugadzira

- Mirayiro yemumba inofanira kushanda pasina indaneti.
- Chinogadzirisa chinangwa chemuno ndicho chinotanga kushandiswa kupfuura maLLM.
- Inzwi nehurukuro dzakavanzika zvinofanira kugadziriswa munharaunda pazvinogoneka.
- Mienzaniso yemugore inoshandiswa chete pamibvunzo yakakumbirwa pachena kana yakaoma, uye chete kana mushandisi akasarudza izvi pakugadzira mubatsiri.
- Hurongwa hunofanira kuchengetedza kupisa kwemidziyo nenguva yekupindura zvakatsiga kana huchishanda nguva dzose.

## Mamiriro echirongwa

Chirongwa ichi chakatsanangurwa segwaro remabasa achaitwa. Zvirongwa zvakananga, mabasa anoitwa otomatiki uye zvinyorwa zvekuisa hurongwa zvichawedzerwa sezvo zvikumbiro zvevashandisi zvichipedzwa.
