# Umsizi wasekhaya ohlakaniphile osebenzisa izilimi eziningi

Le phrojekthi ichaza ukwakhiwa kohlelo lwamadivayisi asekhaya olusebenza endaweni yakini ku-Raspberry Pi, kanye nomsizi wezwi oqonda izilimi eziningi.

## Umsuka wegama elithi Mina

**Mina** lisuselwa egameni lesiJalimane sakudala *minna*, elisho “inkumbulo”, “uthando” noma “into ekhunjulwayo”. Leli gama likhona nakwezinye izilimi: ngesiPheresiya, *mīnā* lihlobene “nokukhanya” noma “ingilazi eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka”; ngesiJapane, *mina* lisho “wonke umuntu” noma “sonke ndawonye”.

Lezi zincazelo zimelela **inkumbulo esabelana ngayo nomphakathi**, okuyizimiso ezihambisana nomsizi oseka impilo yansuku zonke yasekhaya.

Ngokomsindo, uMina yigama elithambile kodwa elicacile. Libizwa ngezinhlamvu zamagama `Mi-na`, futhi mancane amathuba okuthi liphanjaniswe namagama avamile asetshenziswa ekhaya.

## Inhloso

Ukwakha uhlelo lwasekhaya oluvikela ubumfihlo, oluthembekile nolusheshayo, oluvumela ukulawula amadivayisi asekhaya ngemiyalo yezwi ngaphandle kokuphoqeleka ukuncika ezinsizeni zamafu.

Ukwakhiwa kwalo kuhlanganisa:

- Ukucutshungulwa kwedatha endaweni yakini ukuze kwenziwe imiyalo yasekhaya futhi kuvikelwe ubumfihlo.
- Ubuhlakani bokwenziwa bendawo obusebenzisa i-Ollama ukuphendula imibuzo ejwayelekile.
- Amamodeli amafu emibuzo eyinkimbinkimbi nolwazi lwakamuva.

## Ihadiwe

- I-Raspberry Pi 5 ene-RAM engu-8 GB iyatuswa. Ukuhambisana kunomkhawulo kuze kufike ku-Raspberry Pi 3B+.
- I-NVMe SSD, ngaphandle kokusebenzisa ikhadi le-SD. Ukuhambisana nekhadi le-SD kunomkhawulo.
- Imakrofoni nesipikha se-USB esikwazi ukuthumela nokwamukela umsindo.
- Amadivayisi asekhaya ahambisana ne-Zigbee, Matter, Philips Hue noma i-Wi-Fi yasendaweni.
- Ukupholisa okusebenzayo ukuze ukushisa kunganciphisi ukusebenza.

## Ukwakhiwa kohlelo

1. I-Home Assistant OS ilawula amadivayisi nemisebenzi ezenzakalelayo.
2. I-openWakeWord ithola igama lokuvusa umsizi endaweni yakini.
3. I-Whisper ibhala imiyalo ngolimi olusebenzayo isebenzisa i-Wyoming.
4. Umhleli wezinhloso wenza imiyalo yasekhaya endaweni yakini.
5. I-Piper ikhiqiza izimpendulo zezwi ngesiCatalan.
6. I-Ollama iphendula imibuzo ejwayelekile ingathumeli idatha ku-inthanethi.
7. Ubuhlakani bokwenziwa bamafu buthola kuphela imibuzo ecelwe ngokusobala umsebenzisi edinga i-AI ethuthukile.

## Izici ezihleliwe

- Ukulawula amalambu, amasokhethi, okokushisa noma ukupholisa, amakhethini namanye amadivayisi ngezwi.
- Amagama ahlukile ngezilimi eziningi.
- Izibali-sikhathi ezilawulwa ngezwi.
- Ukubuza ngesimo sezulu sendawo.
- Ukudlala nokulawula umculo.
- Enye indlela eyimfihlo esebenzisa imodeli ye-Ollama.
- Ukuhlonza umuntu ngezwi lakhe.
- Ukugcina inkumbulo yomongo wesikhathi eside.
- Ukukhetha ukudlulisela imibuzo kumodeli ye-AI yamafu.
- Amakhophi ayisipele nokuqapha ukusebenza.

## Izimiso zokwakha

- Imiyalo yasekhaya kufanele isebenze ngaphandle koxhumano lwe-inthanethi.
- Umhleli wezinhloso wasendaweni uza kuqala kunama-LLM.
- Umsindo nezingxoxo eziyimfihlo kucutshungulwa endaweni yakini uma kungenzeka.
- Amamodeli amafu asetshenziswa kuphela emibuzweni ecelwe ngokusobala noma eyinkimbinkimbi, futhi kuphela uma umsebenzisi evumele lokho ngokucacile ngesikhathi ehlela umsizi.
- Uhlelo kufanele lugcine izinga lokushisa nesikhathi sokuphendula kuzinzile uma lusebenza ngokuqhubekayo.

## Isimo sephrojekthi

Le phrojekthi ichazwe njengohlu lwemisebenzi okufanele yenziwe. Izilungiselelo ezithile, imisebenzi ezenzakalelayo nemibhalo yokufaka uhlelo kuzongezwa njengoba izidingo zabasebenzisi ziqedwa.
