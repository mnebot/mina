# Umufasha w'urugo rw'ikoranabuhanga mu ndimi nyinshi

Uyu mushinga usobanura uko hubakwa urusobe rw'ibikoresho byo mu rugo bikorera mu rugo kuri Raspberry Pi, hamwe n'umufasha w'ijwi ushobora gukoresha indimi nyinshi.

## Inkomoko y'izina Mina

**Mina** rikomoka ku ijambo rya kera ryo mu kidage *minna*, risobanura “kwibuka”, “urukundo” cyangwa “icyibukwa”. Iri zina riboneka no mu zindi ndimi: mu giperisi, *mīnā* rifitanye isano n’“urumuri” cyangwa “ikirahuri cy'ubururu”; mu kiyapani, *mina* risobanura “bose” cyangwa “twese hamwe”.

Izi nyito zigereranya **kwibuka dusangiye n'ubusabane**, indangagaciro zihuye n'umufasha utera inkunga ubuzima bwa buri munsi mu rugo.

Mu buryo bw'uko ryumvikana, Mina ni izina ryoroshye ariko risobanutse. Rivugwa mu myanya ibiri `Mi-na`, kandi ntirishobora kwitiranywa cyane n'amagambo asanzwe akoreshwa mu rugo.

## Intego

Kubaka sisitemu y'urugo rw'ikoranabuhanga irinda ibanga, yizewe kandi yihuta, ituma ibikoresho byo mu rugo bigenzurwa hakoreshejwe amategeko y'ijwi, bitabaye ngombwa kwishingikiriza kuri serivisi zo ku rubuga.

Imiterere yayo ihuza:

- Gutunganyiriza amakuru mu rugo kugira ngo amategeko y'urugo akorwe kandi ibanga ririndwe.
- Ubwenge bw'ikoranabuhanga bukorera mu rugo bukoresha Ollama mu gusubiza ibibazo rusange.
- Moderi zo ku rubuga zikoreshwa ku bibazo bigoye no ku makuru agezweho.

## Ibikoresho

- Raspberry Pi 5 ifite RAM ya 8 GB ni yo isabwa. Ihuzwa ry'ibikoresho rigarukira kuri Raspberry Pi 3B+.
- Disiki ya NVMe SSD, hatifashishijwe ikarita ya SD. Ihuzwa n'ikarita ya SD rifite aho rigarukira.
- Mikoro na ruhururamajwi za USB zishobora kwakira no kohereza amajwi.
- Ibikoresho byo mu rugo bihuye na Zigbee, Matter, Philips Hue cyangwa Wi-Fi yo mu rugo.
- Igikoresho gikora gikonjesha kugira ngo ubushyuhe butagabanya umuvuduko w'imikorere.

## Imiterere ya sisitemu

1. Home Assistant OS igenzura ibikoresho n'imirimo yikora.
2. openWakeWord imenya ijambo ryo gukangura umufasha mu rugo.
3. Whisper ihindura amategeko yavuzwe akandika mu rurimi rukoreshwa, ikoresheje Wyoming.
4. Utunganya intego akorera amategeko y'urugo mu rugo.
5. Piper akora ibisubizo by'ijwi mu Gikatalani.
6. Ollama asubiza ibibazo rusange atohereje amakuru kuri interineti.
7. Ubwenge bwo ku rubuga buhabwa gusa ibibazo umukoresha yasabye ku mugaragaro kandi bisaba AI ihanitse.

## Ibikorwa biteganyijwe

- Gukoresha ijwi mu kugenzura amatara, amashanyarazi, ibyuma bishyushya cyangwa bikonjesha, amarido n'ibindi bikoresho.
- Amazina y'inyongera mu ndimi nyinshi.
- Igihe gipimwa kandi kigenzurwa n'ijwi.
- Kubaza uko ikirere kimeze mu karere.
- Gucuranga no kugenzura umuziki.
- Ubundi buryo bw'ibanga bukoresha moderi ya Ollama.
- Kumenya umuntu hakoreshejwe ijwi rye.
- Kwibuka ibiganiro n'imiterere yabyo mu gihe kirekire.
- Kohereza ikibazo kuri moderi ya AI yo ku rubuga ari uko bibaye ngombwa.
- Kubika amakopi y'umutekano no gukurikirana imikorere.

## Amahame agenga igishushanyo mbonera

- Amategeko y'urugo agomba gukora na interineti idahari.
- Utunganya intego wo mu rugo ni we uhabwa umwanya wa mbere kurusha LLM.
- Amajwi n'ibiganiro by'ibanga bitunganyirizwa mu rugo uko bishoboka.
- Moderi zo ku rubuga zikoreshwa gusa ku bibazo byasabwe ku mugaragaro cyangwa bigoye, kandi ari uko umukoresha abyemeye mu gihe cyo gushyiraho umufasha.
- Sisitemu igomba gutuma ubushyuhe n'igihe cyo gutegereza igisubizo bihama nubwo ikomeza gukora.

## Uko umushinga uhagaze

Umushinga wasobanuwe nk'urutonde rw'imirimo iteganyijwe. Igenamiterere ryihariye, imirimo yikora n'inyandiko zishyiraho sisitemu bizongerwaho uko ibikorwa by'abakoresha birangira.
