# Umncedisi wasekhaya okrelekrele osebenzisa iilwimi ezininzi

Le projekthi ichaza ukwakhiwa kwenkqubo yezixhobo zasekhaya esebenza ekuhlaleni kwi-Raspberry Pi, kunye nomncedisi welizwi oziqondayo iilwimi ezininzi.

## Imvelaphi yegama elithi Mina

**Mina** lisuka kwigama lesiJamani sakudala *minna*, elithetha “inkumbulo”, “uthando” okanye “into ekhunjulwayo”. Eli gama likho nakwezinye iilwimi: ngesiPersi, *mīnā* linxulunyaniswa “nokukhanya” okanye “iglasi eluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka”; ngesiJapan, *mina* lithetha “wonke umntu” okanye “sonke sikunye”.

Ezi ntsingiselo zimele **inkumbulo ekwabelwana ngayo noluntu**, imigaqo ehambelana nomncedisi oxhasa ubomi bemihla ngemihla ekhaya.

Ngokwesandi, uMina ligama elithambileyo nelicacileyo. Libizwa ngokwamalungu `Mi-na`, yaye akunakwenzeka kakhulu ukuba lididaniswe namagama aqhelekileyo asetyenziswa ekhaya.

## Injongo

Ukwakha inkqubo yasekhaya ekhusela iimfihlo, ethembekileyo nekhawulezayo, evumela ukulawula izixhobo zasekhaya ngemiyalelo yelizwi ngaphandle kokunyanzelwa kukuxhomekeka kwiinkonzo zelifu.

Uyilo ludibanisa:

- Ukulungiswa kwedatha ekuhlaleni ukuze kuphunyezwe imiyalelo yasekhaya kuze kukhuselwe iimfihlo.
- Ubukrelekrele bokwenziwa basekuhlaleni obusebenzisa i-Ollama ukuphendula imibuzo ngokubanzi.
- Iimodeli zelifu zemibuzo entsonkothileyo neenkcukacha ezihlaziyiweyo.

## Izixhobo zekhompyutha

- I-Raspberry Pi 5 ene-RAM eyi-8 GB iyacetyiswa. Ukuhambelana kuncinane ukuya kwi-Raspberry Pi 3B+.
- I-NVMe SSD, ngaphandle kokusebenzisa ikhadi le-SD. Ukuhambelana nekhadi le-SD kulinganiselwe.
- Imakrofoni nesandisi-lizwi se-USB esisebenza macala omabini.
- Izixhobo zasekhaya ezihambelana ne-Zigbee, Matter, Philips Hue okanye i-Wi-Fi yasekhaya.
- Ukupholisa okusebenzayo ukuze ubushushu bungacothisi inkqubo.

## Uyilo lwenkqubo

1. I-Home Assistant OS ilawula izixhobo nemisebenzi ezenzekelayo.
2. I-openWakeWord ibona igama lokuvusa umncedisi ekuhlaleni.
3. I-Whisper ibhala imiyalelo kulwimi olusebenzayo isebenzisa i-Wyoming.
4. Umqhubekisi weenjongo uphumeza imiyalelo yasekhaya ekuhlaleni.
5. I-Piper ivelisa iimpendulo zelizwi ngesiCatalan.
6. I-Ollama iphendula imibuzo ngokubanzi ingathumeli datha kwi-intanethi.
7. I-AI yelifu ifumana kuphela imibuzo ekhethwe ngumsebenzisi ngokucacileyo efuna i-AI ekumgangatho ophezulu.

## Iimpawu ezicwangcisiweyo

- Ukulawula izibane, iisokethi, izixhobo zokufudumeza okanye zokupholisa, amakhethini nezinye izixhobo ngelizwi.
- Amagama athatha indawo yamanye ngeelwimi ezininzi.
- Izibali-xesha ezilawulwa ngelizwi.
- Ukubuza ngemozulu yendawo.
- Ukudlala nokulawula umculo.
- Enye indlela yabucala esebenzisa imodeli ye-Ollama.
- Ukuchonga umntu ngelizwi lakhe.
- Ukugcina umxholo wencoko ixesha elide.
- Ukukhetha ukuthumela imibuzo kwimodeli ye-AI yelifu.
- Iikopi zogcino nokubeka iliso kwindlela esebenza ngayo inkqubo.

## Imigaqo yoyilo

- Imiyalelo yasekhaya kufuneka isebenze ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.
- Umqhubekisi weenjongo wasekuhlaleni uza kuqala kune-LLM.
- Ilizwi neencoko zabucala zicutshungulwa ekuhlaleni nanini na kunokwenzeka.
- Iimodeli zelifu zisetyenziswa kuphela kwimibuzo ecelwe ngokucacileyo okanye entsonkothileyo, yaye kuphela xa umsebenzisi ekhethe oko ngokucacileyo xa elungiselela umncedisi.
- Inkqubo kufuneka igcine ubushushu nexesha lokuphendula zizinzile xa isebenza ngokuqhubekayo.

## Imeko yeprojekthi

Le projekthi ichazwe njengoluhlu lwemisebenzi ekufuneka yenziwe. Uqwalaselo oluthile, imisebenzi ezenzekelayo nezikripthi zokufakela ziya kongezwa njengoko amabali abasebenzisi egqitywa.
