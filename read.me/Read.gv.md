# Cooneyder thie ymm-lhengagh

Ta’n phobbal shoh cur shickyrys er strooshaght cooneyder ghoo ymm-lhengagh er Raspberry Pi sy ynnyd.

## Bun enmys Mina

Ta fraimheyr Germaanagh shenn ’sy **Mina** ayns *minna*, ta shen gra “cooinaghtyn”, “graih” ny “cloie”. T’eh ayns çhengaghyn elley neesht: ayns Phersish, ta *mīnā* cochianglt rish “sollys” ny “glonney gorrym”, as ayns Shapaanish, ta *mina* cur “yn slane phobble” ny “ooilley cooidjagh”.

Ta ny tushteyyn shoh soilshaghey **cooinaghtyn y phobble as pobblaght**, feyshtyn ta cochianglt lesh cooneyder t’eh cooney lesh bea thie y ghra.

Son yn sheean, ta Mina enmys bog agh soilsheanagh, lesh fuaimlagh `Mi-na` as aght neuchoyrtagh beg rish fockleyn cadjin ayns thieyn.

## Obbraght

Cur seose coraag privaadjagh, firrinagh as tappee as ta cur kied da ynnysyn thie ve reilt lesh sareyghyn ghoo, gyn reddyraghtyn obligaghit da sharbeishyn sniaghtey.

T’eh cur y chochymys shoh cooidjagh:

- Proccesal ynnyd er shennaghyn thie as privaadjys.
- Ommyr ynnyd lesh Ollama er feyshtyn cadjin.
- Moddylyn cloud er feyshtyn doillee as fys noagh.

## Crua-earragh

- Raspberry Pi 5 lesh 8 GB RAM er ynrey. Cummalagh beg dys Raspberry Pi 3B+.
- SSD NVMe gyn cairt SD. Cummalagh beg lesh cairt SD.
- Meechrophone USB y veagh it as speekyr.
- Injyn thie cochianglt rish Zigbee, Matter, Philips Hue ny Wi-Fi ynnyd.
- Ayr ghowaltys dy lhiggey sheese dossan tharmeilagh.

## Ard-ollan

1. T’eh Home Assistant OS stiurey ynnydyn as oardaghyn.
2. Ta openWakeWord faghid yn fockle cur er ynnyd.
3. Ta Whisper transcribey sareyghyn ayns yn çhengey bioit trooid Wyoming.
4. Ta’n proccessor kiangley cur sareyghyn thie er ynnyd.
5. Ta Piper cur freggyrtyn ayns Gaelg.
6. Ta Ollama cur freggyrt er feyshtyn cadjin gyn data ve currit da’n eddyr-voggyl.
7. Cha vel cloud AI goaill agh feyshtyn explicit ta feme AI cadlagh.

## Cowraghyn kiarit

- Stiurey ghoo er sollysyn, pluggyn, heeallagh, blinds as gronney.
- Aliasyn ymm-lhengagh.
- Ta timeryn stiurit lesh ghoo.
- Feyshtyn y ghrian ynnyd.
- Cur seose as stiurey kiaullee.
- Fallback privaadjagh da moddel Ollama.
- Coonrey yn dooinney lurg ghoo.
- Cooinaghtyn-context liurid.
- Cur er moddel AI cloud as roghyn.
- Coadey as scrutaght yn oyr.

## Bun-prinseebyn cummal

- Lhisagh sareyghyn thie obbraghey gyn eddyr-voggyl.
- She’n proccessor kiangley ynnyd yn raad ainsh as ta LLMyn gyn ard.
- Lhisagh claashtyn feyshtyn as cooish privaadjagh ve proccessit syn ynnyd.
- Cha vel moddylyn cloud currit er agh son feyshtyn explicit ny doillee, as eh er vriaghtit ec yn ymmydeyr tra ta’n cooneyder settit.
- Lhisagh’n coraag reill teawpettyryn as moalidyn sheeanit fo earroo slane.

## Stayd y phobball

Ta’n phobbal y chum ymyrkagh backlog. Lhisagh shickyraghyn, oardaghyn as scriptes lhiantyn ve cur rish as reddyraghtyn ymmydeyr cur rish.
