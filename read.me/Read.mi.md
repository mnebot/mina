# Kaiāwhina kāinga atamai reo-maha

**Reo:** Māori

**Pānuihia ki:** [Te reo Māori](Read.mi.md) · [English](Read.en.md) · [Català](Read.ca.md) · [Español](Read.es.md) · [Français](Read.fr.md) · [Deutsch](Read.de.md) · [Italiano](Read.it.md) · [Português](Read.pt.md) · [Nederlands](Read.nl.md)

E whakaahua ana tēnei kaupapa i te whakatūnga o tētahi pūnaha kāinga atamai ā-rohe ki runga i tētahi Raspberry Pi, me tētahi kaiāwhina reo mō ngā reo maha.

## Te takenga mai o te ingoa Mina

He pūtake tawhito tō **Mina** i te kupu Tiamana onamata *minna*, arā, «maumahara», «aroha» rānei «maharatanga». Kei ētahi atu reo hoki: i te reo Pāhia, e pā ana a *mīnā* ki te «mārama» ki te «karāhe kahurangi» rānei; i te reo Hapanihi, ko te tikanga o *mina* ko «te katoa» rānei «katoa tahi».

E tohu ana ēnei whakamāramatanga i te **maumahara ngātahi me te hapori**, he uara e hāngai ana ki tētahi kaiāwhina e haere tahi ana, e tautoko ana hoki i te oranga o ia rā i te kāinga.

He māeneene, he mārama te tangi o Mina: e rua ngā wāhanga oro, `Mi-na`, ā, he iti te tūpono ka rangiruatia ki ngā kupu e whakamahia ana i te kāinga.

## Te whāinga

Ko te hanga i tētahi pūnaha kāinga atamai tūmataiti, pono, tere hoki, kia taea ai te whakahaere i ngā taputapu o te kāinga mā ngā whakahau reo, me te kore e herea ki ngā ratonga kapua.

Ka whakakotahi te hoahoanga i ēnei:

- Te tukatuka ā-rohe mō ngā whakahau kāinga atamai me te tūmataitinga.
- Te mātauranga ā-rohe mā Ollama mō ngā pātai whānui.
- Ngā tauira kapua mō ngā pātai uaua me ngā mōhiohio hou.

## Taputapu

- E taunakitia ana te Raspberry Pi 5 me te 8 GB RAM. He iti noa te hototahitanga ki te Raspberry Pi 3B+.
- He kōpae SSD NVMe, kāore he kāri SD e whakamahia ana. He iti te hototahitanga ki te kāri SD.
- He hopuoro USB me tētahi kaikōrero e rua ngā aronga.
- He taputapu kāinga atamai e hototahi ana ki Zigbee, Matter, Philips Hue, ki te Wi-Fi ā-rohe rānei.
- He whakamātao hohe kia kore ai te wera e whakaiti i te tere o te pūnaha.

## Hoahoanga

1. Mā Home Assistant OS ngā taputapu me ngā aunoatanga e whakahaere.
2. Ka kite ā-rohe a openWakeWord i te kupu whakaoho.
3. Ka tuhi a Whisper i ngā whakahau ki te reo hohe mā Wyoming.
4. Ka whakatinana ā-rohe te pūtukatuka hiahia i ngā whakahau kāinga atamai.
5. Ka whakahua a Piper i ngā whakautu ki te reo Kātārana.
6. Ka whakautu a Ollama i ngā pātai whānui me te kore tuku raraunga ki te Ipurangi.
7. Ka whiwhi te AI kapua i ngā pātai kua tonoa māriretia, ina hiahiatia he AI matatau.

## Ngā āheinga kua whakamaheretia

- Te whakahaere ā-reo i ngā rama, ngā mono hiko, te whakamahana, ngā ārai matapihi me ētahi atu taputapu.
- Ngā ingoa kē mō ngā reo maha.
- Ngā matawā mā te reo.
- Te uiui mō te huarere ā-rohe.
- Te whakatangi me te whakahaere waiata.
- He kōwhiringa tūmataiti mā tētahi tauira Ollama.
- Te tautuhi tangata mā te reo.
- Te maumahara horopaki mō te wā roa.
- Te tuku kōwhiringa ki tētahi tauira AI kapua.
- Ngā pūrua me te aroturuki mahi.

## Ngā mātāpono hoahoa

- Me mahi ngā whakahau kāinga atamai ahakoa kāore he Ipurangi.
- Ko te pūtukatuka hiahia ā-rohe te mea matua i mua i ngā LLM.
- Me tukatuka ā-rohe ngā raraunga oro me ngā kōrero tūmataiti i te tuatahi.
- Ka whakamahia ngā tauira kapua mō ngā pātai kua tonoa māriretia, mō ngā pātai uaua rānei, ā, mēnā kua kōwhiria māriretia e te kaiwhakamahi i te wā whakarite i te kaiāwhina.
- Me mau tonu te pūmautanga o te pāmahana me te tōmuri whakautu i raro i te kawenga haere tonu.

## Te tūnga o te kaupapa

Kua tautuhia te kaupapa hei rārangi mahi. Ka tāpirihia ngā whirihoranga motuhake, ngā aunoatanga me ngā tuhinga whakatinana i te otinga haere o ngā kōrero kaiwhakamahi.
