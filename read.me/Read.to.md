# Tokoni ki he ʻapi poto ʻi he ngaahi lea kehekehe

**Lea:** Faka-Tonga

**Lau ʻi he:** [Faka-Tonga](Read.to.md) · [English](Read.en.md) · [Català](Read.ca.md) · [Español](Read.es.md) · [Français](Read.fr.md) · [Deutsch](Read.de.md) · [Italiano](Read.it.md) · [Português](Read.pt.md) · [Nederlands](Read.nl.md)

ʻOku fakamatalaʻi ʻe he ngāue ni ʻa hono fokotuʻu ʻo ha polokalama ʻapi poto fakalotofonua ʻi ha Raspberry Pi, mo ha tokoni leʻo ʻoku ne lava ʻo lea ʻi he ngaahi lea kehekehe.

## Ko e tupuʻanga ʻo e hingoa Mina

ʻOku tupu ʻa **Mina** mei he foʻi lea Siamane motuʻa *minna*, ko hono ʻuhinga ko e «manatu», «ʻofa» pe «meʻa ke manatuʻi». ʻOku toe ʻi he ngaahi lea kehe: ʻi he lea faka-Pēsia, ʻoku fekauʻaki ʻa *mīnā* mo e «maama» pe «ʻiloʻilo lanu moana»; ʻi he lea faka-Siapani, ʻoku ʻuhinga ʻa *mina* ki he «tokotaha kotoa» pe «fakataha kotoa».

ʻOku fakaʻilongaʻi ʻe he ngaahi ʻuhinga ko ʻení ʻa e **manatu fakataha mo e komiuniti**, ʻa ia ʻoku feʻunga mo ha tokoni ʻoku ne ʻalu fakataha mo poupou ki he moʻui fakaʻaho ʻi ʻapi.

ʻOku ongo molū kae mahino ʻa e hingoa Mina: ʻoku leaʻi ʻi he ongo silapa, `Mi-na`, pea ʻoku siʻi ʻa e faingamālie ke fetoʻoaki mo e ngaahi lea angamaheni ʻo e ʻapi.

## Taumuʻa

Ke faʻu ha polokalama ʻapi poto fakataautaha, falalaʻanga mo vave, ke lava ʻo puleʻi ʻa e ngaahi meʻangāue ʻi ʻapi ʻaki ʻa e ngaahi fekau leʻo ʻo ʻikai fakafalala pau ki he ngaahi ngāue kapua.

ʻOku fakatahaʻi ʻe he fokotuʻutuʻu:

- Fakahoko fakalotofonua ki he ngaahi fekau ʻapi poto mo e maluʻi fakamatala.
- ʻIlo fakalotofonua fakafou ʻi Ollama ki he ngaahi fehuʻi fakalukufua.
- Ngaahi sipinga kapua ki he ngaahi fehuʻi faingataʻa mo e fakamatala foʻou.

## Meʻa fakakomipiuta

- ʻOku fokotuʻu ʻa e Raspberry Pi 5 mo e 8 GB RAM. ʻOku fakangatangata ʻa e fehokotakiʻanga ki he Raspberry Pi 3B+.
- Meʻa tāmateʻanga SSD NVMe, taʻe ngāueʻaki ha kātisi SD. ʻOku fakangatangata ʻa e fehokotakiʻanga mo e kātisi SD.
- Maikofoni USB mo ha meʻa fakaongo ʻoku ngāue ʻi he ongo tafaʻaki.
- Ngaahi meʻangāue ʻapi poto ʻoku fenāpasi mo Zigbee, Matter, Philips Hue pe Wi-Fi fakalotofonua.
- Fakamokomoko ʻoku ngāue ke taʻofi ʻa e vela lahi mo e holo ʻo e vave ngāue.

## Fokotuʻutuʻu

1. ʻOku puleʻi ʻe Home Assistant OS ʻa e ngaahi meʻangāue mo e ngaahi ngāue fakaʻotomētiki.
2. ʻOku ʻiloʻi fakalotofonua ʻe openWakeWord ʻa e lea kamata.
3. ʻOku liliu ʻe Whisper ʻa e ngaahi fekau ki he lea ʻoku lolotonga ngāueʻaki ʻo fakafou ʻi Wyoming.
4. ʻOku fakahoko fakalotofonua ʻe he meʻangāue ki he ngaahi taumuʻa ʻa e ngaahi fekau ʻapi poto.
5. ʻOku lea ʻa Piper ʻaki ʻa e ngaahi tali ʻi he lea faka-Katālani.
6. ʻOku tali ʻe Ollama ʻa e ngaahi fehuʻi fakalukufua taʻe ʻave ha fakamatala ki he ʻInitaneti.
7. ʻOku maʻu ʻe he AI kapua ʻa e ngaahi fehuʻi kuo kole mahino pē ʻoku fiemaʻu ai ʻa e AI fakalakalaka.

## Ngaahi meʻa ʻoku palaniʻi

- Puleʻi ʻaki ʻa e leʻo ʻa e ngaahi maama, palaki, ʻea, puipui matapā mo e ngaahi meʻangāue kehe.
- Ngaahi hingoa kehe ʻi he ngaahi lea kehekehe.
- Ngaahi taimi kuo puleʻi ʻaki ʻa e leʻo.
- Fehuʻi ki he tuʻunga ʻo e ʻea fakalotofonua.
- Fakamimilo mo puleʻi ʻa e hiva.
- Founga malu fakataautaha ʻaki ha sipinga Ollama.
- ʻIloʻi ʻa e tokotaha ʻaki hono leʻo.
- Manatu ki he puipuituʻa ʻo e talanoa ʻi ha taimi lōloa.
- Fili ke fakafononga ki ha sipinga AI kapua.
- Ngaahi tatau malu mo e tokangaʻi ʻo e fakahoko.

## Ngaahi tefitoʻi meʻa ki he palani

- Kuo pau ke ngāue ʻa e ngaahi fekau ʻapi poto neongo ʻoku ʻikai ha ʻInitaneti.
- ʻOku tomuʻa ngāue ʻa e meʻangāue fakalotofonua ki he ngaahi taumuʻa kimuʻa ʻi he LLM.
- ʻOku lelei ange ke fakahoko fakalotofonua ʻa e ngaahi fakamatala leʻo mo e ngaahi talanoa fakataautaha.
- ʻOku ngāueʻaki ʻa e ngaahi sipinga kapua ki he ngaahi fehuʻi kuo kole mahino pe faingataʻa, pea kapau pē kuo fili mahino ʻe he tokotaha ngāue ʻi hono fokotuʻu ʻo e tokoni.
- Kuo pau ke tauhi maʻu ʻe he polokalama ʻa e mafana mo e taimi tali ʻi he ngāue hokohoko.

## Tuʻunga ʻo e ngāue

Kuo fakamatalaʻi ʻa e ngāue ni ko ha backlog ngāue. ʻE tānaki atu ʻa e ngaahi fokotuʻutuʻu pau, ngaahi ngāue fakaʻotomētiki mo e ngaahi script fokotuʻu lolotonga hono fakakakato ʻo e ngaahi talanoa ʻa e tokotaha ngāue.
